🍽 Restaurants in Interlaken

Our personal picks for eating out in Interlaken — a mix of Swiss classics, Italian favourites and international gems, all within walking distance of the hotel.

🇨🇭 Swiss
Röschtigrabe
Swiss · Rösti · Bar
📍 Marktgasse 4 · 20 min walk
A lively spot specialising in rösti and Swiss classics with craft cocktails and a great atmosphere. Closed Wednesdays for lunch and Sundays.
Website Maps
Restaurant Stadthaus
Swiss · Fondue · Rösti
📍 Untere Gasse 2, Unterseen · 25 min walk
A 200-year-old building in the charming old town of Unterseen. Excellent fondue, rösti and Swiss classics with local beers on tap. Worth the walk.
Website Maps
Restaurant Bären
Swiss · Fondue · Traditional
📍 Seestrasse 2, Unterseen · 25 min walk
A historic wooden restaurant dating to the 17th century with views over the Aare. Famous for cheese fondue and raclette. Closed Monday & Tuesday.
Website Maps
Vintage Chuchichästli
Swiss · American Fusion
📍 Unterseen · 25 min walk
A warm, family-run restaurant with a Swiss-American menu using regional ingredients. Consistently praised for food quality, value and friendly service.
Maps
🍕 Italian
Sapori
Italian · Pizza · Pasta
📍 Höheweg 41 · 12 min walk
Michelin-listed Italian inside the Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel. Stone-baked pizza, fresh pasta and a beautiful mountain terrace. A real treat.
Website Maps
Il Buongustaio
Italian · Pizza · Pasta
📍 Marktgasse 48 · 20 min walk
A hidden gem with a 4.9 Google rating — authentic Neapolitan pizza on Lievito Madre dough and fresh pasta. No reservations, go early. Evenings only, closed Sundays.
Maps
🍜 Asian
Little Thai
Thai
📍 Hauptstrasse 19, Matten · 22 min walk
A family-run gem serving fresh, authentic Thai food — great curries, pad thai and spring rolls. Highly rated and popular. Closed Mon & Tue.
Website Maps
Simply Sushi
Japanese · Sushi
📍 Marktgasse 17 · 20 min walk
Excellent sushi with creative rolls alongside ramen and udon noodle soups. A cosy spot in the centre of town. Open Tuesday to Saturday.
Website Maps
🍛 Indian
Lal Qila
Indian · Pakistani · Halal
📍 Untere Bönigstrasse 6 · 3 min walk
Authentic Indian and Pakistani cuisine right by the hotel — praised for chicken tikka masala, daal tarka, mix grill and naan. Halal. Outdoor seating available.
Maps
Spice India
Indian
📍 Postgasse 6 · 18 min walk
Authentic Indian curries, naan and a good range of vegetarian options. A welcome change of pace with friendly service.
Maps
🥙 Middle Eastern
Layaly Beirut
Lebanese
📍 Jungfraustrasse · 15 min walk
Authentic Lebanese kitchen with mezze platters, shawarma and falafel. Great for sharing and very popular with locals and visitors alike.
Maps
🌍 International
Hüsi Bierhaus
Craft Beer · Burgers · Swiss
📍 Postgasse 3 · 18 min walk
A beloved local institution with house-brewed craft beers, hearty food and a lively atmosphere. One of the most popular spots in town — book ahead at weekends.
Website Maps
Asllani's Corner
Burgers · American
📍 Höheweg 90 · 5 min walk
One of the most popular burger spots in Interlaken — gourmet burgers, truffle fries and generous portions. Busy at weekends, worth booking ahead.
Website Maps
El Azteca
Mexican
📍 Jungfraustrasse · 15 min walk
Probably the only Mexican restaurant in the Bernese Oberland — great fajitas, guacamole and margaritas. Fun and relaxed atmosphere.

📋 House Rules

No Cooking or Eating in Room

  • Cooking in rooms is strictly prohibited.
  • The kettle is for hot drinks only — not for cooking food.
  • We kindly ask guests not to eat meals in their rooms.
  • Food deliveries to rooms are not permitted.

Bathroom

  • Always keep the shower curtain inside the bath when showering — there is no floor drain.
  • Water damage caused by flooding will be charged to the guest.
  • Do not flush wet wipes or sanitary products — use the bin provided.

Smoking

  • Strictly no smoking inside the hotel.
  • Balcony smoking is permitted.
  • CHF 250 deep-clean fee applies if smoking indoors.

Quiet Hours

  • Quiet hours: 22:00 – 07:00. Please keep noise to a minimum.
  • Parties or gatherings in rooms are not permitted.

Breakfast

  • Breakfast is not included in the room rate — CHF 18.00 per person per day.
  • Served daily 07:00 – 10:30. Please book with reception the evening before.

Keys

  • Please return both your room key and safety box key to reception on departure.
  • Charges apply for lost or unreturned keys.

Check-out

  • Please vacate your room by 11:00 on your day of departure.
  • Late check-out available on request, subject to availability.
