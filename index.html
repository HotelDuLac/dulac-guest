📋 House Rules

Quiet Hours

  • Quiet hours: 22:00 – 07:00. Please keep noise to a minimum.
  • Parties or gatherings in rooms are not permitted.

Bathroom

  • Always keep the shower curtain inside the bath when showering — there is no floor drain.
  • Water damage caused by flooding will be charged to the guest.
  • Do not flush wet wipes or sanitary products — use the bin provided.

Smoking

  • Strictly no smoking inside the hotel.
  • Balcony smoking is permitted.
  • CHF 250 deep-clean fee applies if smoking indoors.

No Cooking in Room

  • Cooking in rooms is strictly prohibited.
  • The kettle is for hot drinks only — not for cooking food.

Keys

  • Please return both your room key and safety box key to reception on departure.
  • Charges apply for lost or unreturned keys.

Breakfast

  • Breakfast is not included in the room rate — CHF 18.00 per person per day.
  • Served daily 07:00 – 10:30. Please book with reception the evening before.

Check-out

  • Please vacate your room by 11:00 on your day of departure.
  • Late check-out available on request, subject to availability.
